# Guitronyeuthuong

Create a professional, modern and responsive Vietnamese website for a student team participating in the DBC2026 Digital Marketing Competition.

Project: Truong Nhan – Ginseng Tea.
Campaign: "Gửi trọn yêu thương".

Website requirements:

Homepage: Display the team name, project name, campaign slogan and an attractive hero banner.

Team introduction: Show team logo, team photo, member names, roles and short biographies.

Project introduction: Introduce Truong Nhan brand, Ginseng Tea product, product benefits and brand story. Do not invent product information.

Marketing campaign: Present the campaign concept, objectives, target audience, key message and 8-week implementation plan.

Marketing activities: Display TikTok videos, Facebook posts, campaign images and links to social media channels.

Contact: Include team contact information and social media links.

Design requirements:

Use an elegant Vietnamese herbal tea aesthetic.

Main colors: dark green, cream, beige and gold.

Professional layout, beautiful typography and high-quality images.

Responsive on desktop and mobile.

Navigation menu with clear sections.

All website content must be in Vietnamese.

Use placeholders for information that has not been provided.

Do not invent team members, statistics, product claims or contact information.

Build a complete, functional website, not just a static mockup.
